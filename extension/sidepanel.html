              Demonstration Scenarios

              Three curated examples demonstrating how ContextLens identifies context nuances. These work offline without an API key.

              Scenario A: Possible Contradiction

              Conference Date Discrepancy

              Two sources give mutually exclusive dates for the same keynote event.

              Claim A: "DevSummit keynote takes place on Friday, Nov 14."
              Evidence B: "All main talks and keynote are confirmed for Saturday, Nov 15."
              Scenario B: Different Context

              Base Price vs. Final Invoice

              Passages report different prices for the same laptop due to excluded taxes & shipping fees.

              Claim A: "The ZenBook Ultra is priced starting at $499."
              Evidence B: "The total order came to $549 for the ZenBook Ultra at checkout."
              Scenario C: Insufficient Context

              Conflicting Youth Employment Stats

              Opposite employment trends without specifying year, region, or age group.

              Claim A: "Youth unemployment increased by 3.8% reaching record levels."
              Evidence B: "Youth joblessness dropped to an all-time low of 7.2%."
              Note: Predefined scenarios are fictional examples created for hackathon demonstration.

              • Zero passive tracking: ContextLens never records browsing history.
              • Explicit user intent: Text is only captured when you click capture or the floating pill.
              • Local storage first: All drafts and research history reside on your local machine.
              • Evidence before conclusions: AI outputs are investigative suggestions, not absolute verdicts.
